## EAMS - Enterprise Assets Management System

The Enterprise Asset Management System is an efficient and intelligent solution designed to enable transparent and standardized management of assets throughout their lifecycle, enhancing operational efficiency and reducing management costs

### Key Benefits

1. Full Lifecycle Asset Management
- Tracks assets from procurement, inventory, usage, maintenance, to disposal
- Supports registration, classification, numbering, and status updates
- Ensures accurate data management across asset lifecycles

2. Real-Time Asset Inventory and Tracking
- Uses barcode / QR code / RFID for fast inventory and asset localization
- Real-time updates on asset location and status
- Prevents loss, redundancy, and underutilization

3. Intelligent Reporting and Data Analysis
- Generates multidimensional reports on utilization, depreciation, and maintenance costs
- Supports customizable reports for agile and informed decision-making

4. Maintenance and Upkeep Management
- Automatic reminders for scheduled maintenance
- Maintains a full maintenance history for compliance and traceability
- Helps extend asset lifespan and reduce downtime

5. Permission and Process Management
- Enables multi-role permission control to safeguard data
- Built-in approval workflows for standardized processes:
  - Allocation
  - Borrowing
  - Disposal

6. Integration and Scalability
- Seamless integration with ERP, financial systems, and other enterprise software
- Open API interfaces for customized development and scalability

7. Applicable Scenarios
- Manufacturing
- Logistics
- Real Estate
- Healthcare
- Information Technology
